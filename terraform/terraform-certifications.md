# Terraform Certifications

## Terraform Associate (004)

Source: https://developer.hashicorp.com/certifications/infrastructure-automation#terraform-associate-(004)-details

### Overview

- **Certification**: HashiCorp Certified: Terraform Associate (004)
- **Product version tested**: Terraform **1.12**
- **Level**: Associate (foundational Terraform knowledge and skills)
- **Exam format**: Multiple-choice
- **Exam length**: ~1 hour

### Exam availability / key dates

Terraform Associate **004** replaces **003** on **January 8, 2026**.

- **January 5, 2026**: Last day to schedule Terraform Associate 003
- **January 7, 2026**: Last day to take Terraform Associate 003
  - If you take 003 at this time and fail, you will need to take the 004 version when you reattempt the exam.
- **January 8, 2026**: First day to take Terraform Associate 004 (Terraform Associate 003 is retired)

Notes from HashiCorp:

- All versions of the Terraform Associate exam validate associate-level skills.
- You may hold both certifications at the same time.
- You can schedule a Terraform Associate 004 exam appointment now.
